<h3 style="text-align: left;">Research interests </h3>

My research focus is developing small-molecule inhibitors to overcome drug resistance in ovarian cancer. My primary research objective is to elucidate the role of glucose metabolism in chemoresistance and identify novel therapeutic targets.
