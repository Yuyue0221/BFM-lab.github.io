Visiting Scholar, Research associate at School of Public Health, Southeast University

Research interests: Cancer metabolism reprogramming and the development of  small molecule inhibitors for gynecological tumors.

Bachelor of Science, RMIT University
Doctor of Philosophy, Tsinghua University

My research focus is developing small-molecule inhibitors to overcome drug resistance in ovarian cancer. My primary research objective is to elucidate the role of glucose metabolism in chemoresistance and identify novel therapeutic targets.
